`biblio/scratchpad/<slug>/activities.md` sits beside every task's other files. It is the task's dated, append-only log and the only place its chronological narrative lives.

```markdown
# activities

- YYYY-MM-DD: <what happened>
```

- One bullet per entry, each starting with its date, appended in date order. Lemongrass reads the task's age from the first date in the file.
- An entry records a decision reached and how, work shipped, or a correction made, never a transcript of each step.
- Entries are never rewritten. What is current belongs in the task's other files.
