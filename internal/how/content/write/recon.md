`biblio/scratchpad/<slug>/recon.md` holds an investigation with no build. It opens with the question being investigated, then a synthesis rewritten in place as understanding firms up, never a log:

- what is settled, with the evidence behind it;
- what is still open.

The chronological narrative goes to `activities.md`. Once settled, the findings become a book chapter (`lgrass how write chapter`), or the task closes with no chapter when the answer is "not worth pursuing".
